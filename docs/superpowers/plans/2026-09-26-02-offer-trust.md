## Review focus

1. Caller substitutes identity or restaurant IDs: ownership remains server-derived.
2. Merchant changes price/end time after a claim: accepted terms/deadline survive.
3. Request is repeated or simultaneous: inventory is neither oversold nor double-counted.
4. Clock equals the deadline exactly: the hold is expired and cannot redeem.
5. Invalid money input: NaN, infinity, negatives and fractional cents are rejected.
