This candidate's removal and packaging guard do not erase existing main, prior
public Git history, clones or previously distributed copies. Existing main still
contains the research until an approved merge. Older retained archives containing
research must remain private. Private preservation alone does not establish
exclusive access, encryption or approved retention. Keep original research and
historical evidence intact pending the remaining decisions.

The retained engineering archives and private synthetic recovery sets do not
decide off-host backup readers, retention or public release. Keep protected
configuration, keys, credentials, photos and private raw logs out of shared
receipts. An operator must choose and
exercise protected off-host backup before hosted recovery can be accepted.

the intended host, then record real inbox, iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and a
small consenting pilot using the exact deployed revision. See
[release gates](RELEASE-GATES.md), [phone testing](PHONE-TESTING.md) and
